Frequently asked

Learning platform FAQs.

Who gets access to the Mentora Edge digital platform?

Every Mentora Edge student, whether enrolled in offline coaching or online personal tuition, receives access as part of the student experience.

Does the platform replace live teaching?

No. The platform is designed as a supporting digital layer around the learning experience. Educators and live teaching remain central.

Are online tuition sessions the same as offline batch timings?

No. Online personal tuition is conducted through dedicated online sessions with schedules separate from the offline batches.

Which classes are covered?

Mentora Edge currently structures its programs around Classes VI–XII, across Middle School, Secondary and Senior Secondary levels.

How can I enrol?

You can explore the available programs and use the registration or enquiry options to begin the enrolment process.
