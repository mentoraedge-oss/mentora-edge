📘 Classes IX–X

Secondary School Preparation

Classes IX and X mark a significant transition in a student's academic journey. Our approach focuses on strengthening concepts while maintaining consistent preparation throughout the academic year.

Students receive structured support in English, Social Science, Science and Maths, along with periodic assessments designed to help identify areas requiring further practice.
